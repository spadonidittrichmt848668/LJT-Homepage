I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

Education
======
* **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024 - Present
  * HKUST NLP Group, a natural language processing research group led by Professor Junxian He
  * Supervisor: Professor Junxian He
* **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), 2020 - 2024
  * Graduated in June 2024
  * Undergraduate studies advised by Professor Junxian He

Research Experience

Research Interests and Skills
======
My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with research interests in:
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
